# Holy Grail: first pullback, frozen FX interpretation

## Evidence and testable claim

Linda Raschke's own hosted [Active Trader March 2004 interview, pages78–79](https://lindaraschke.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/raschke_pt2_0304.pdf) describes ADX(14) above30 on the same timeframe, the first retracement to EMA20, and an expectation of revisiting the recent directional extreme. Her [AIQ August1997 interview](https://lindaraschke.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/august1997.pdf) explains stop entry beyond the previous bar and a newly formed swing-low/high protective stop. It also discusses failed follow-through. The full book chapter has not been independently obtained; these primary interviews support the core without proving every exact Chapter10 instruction. The mechanical episode/reset definition, 3-bar order lifetime, direction rule, RR1 and adverse carry charge below are operational interpretations, not complete reproduction of the book. No quotation is reproduced.

Hypothesis: directional displacement large enough to raise ADX above 30 attracts persistent directional participation. The first EMA20 retracement offers a less extended entry before that participation is exhausted. This is a hypothesis, not an established FX property. Reversal after news, lagging ADX, exhausted trends, prolonged consolidation, and spread relative to the structural stop can reverse the edge. A second hypothesis is that first-only improves PF and average R compared with repeated pullbacks in the same strength episode. Reject if frozen gates fail; do not retune using later-period results.

## Scope fixed before performance

Two adoptable candidates: H1 first pullback and H4 first pullback. Two comparison-only candidates remove first-only at the same timeframes. All have ADX14, EMA20, no higher-timeframe condition, both directions, identical fills, stop and costs. No other filters or optimization. The user default is full RR1:1 exit, which differs from the original discretionary recent-extreme/trailing management. No stop-and-reenter rule. Tests use synthetic prices, not selection data.

## Causal indicators and episode state

Source timestamps are naive Forex Tester time. H1/H4 buckets start at multiples of 3600/14400 seconds from source midnight; no timezone conversion, filling missing bars or Sunday merge. Observed OHLC aggregates only; availability is nominal bucket end. EMA20 is seeded with the first close, then alpha=2/21. TR starts with high-low; ATR14 seeds the mean of first 14 TR at index13. Directional movement uses high/low differences from index1, keeps strictly greater positive movement (ties both zero). Wilder DM and TR means seed indices1..14 at14, then alpha=1/14; DI=100*DM/TR (zero denominator→0). DX=100*abs(DI+-DI-)/(DI++DI-) (zero→0). ADX seeds mean DX14..27 at27, then Wilder recursion. Each phase has fresh episodes/orders/positions but indicators can use only the prefix; 250 observed bars minimum before decisions. No pivots are used and no pivot latency exists.

At close i an episode arms only when ADX[i-1]<=30 and ADX[i]>30, with close above EMA and DI+>DI- (long), or close below EMA and DI->DI+ (short). This strictly implies rising ADX. The arm bar cannot itself be the retracement. Before processing subsequent touches, reset to idle if ADX<25 or episode direction no longer has strict DI dominance. A reset never arms without a fresh 30 upcross. While active, no later upcross replaces the episode. ADX can decline during a pullback.

A touch is low<=EMA<=high, on a later bar. The first touch consumes the first-only opportunity even if its close is not on the trend side, the symbol has an active position/order, or the resulting trade is otherwise skipped. No second touch is substituted. A valid long touch closes above EMA; short below. The repeated-pullback comparison accepts a touch only if the immediately previous bar was wholly above its EMA (long) or wholly below (short); this prevents treating continuous EMA contact as repeated independent pullbacks. For a fair first-only test the first touch also requires that same preceding wholly-away bar; a nonqualifying first touch is still consumed. Both use the same episode reset.

## Orders and execution

At valid touch close, set entry stop at touch high+metadata point (long) or touch low-point (short): the touch bar is the previous confirmed bar when the order becomes active. A causal structural stop replaces the discretionary newly formed swing extreme. Track the latest occurrence of the highest high (long) or lowest low (short) since the arm, using only bars through i-1. The pullback begins at that anchor's next bar and ends at touch i; SL is its minimum low minus one point or maximum high plus one point. Equal directional extremes move the anchor to the latest bar. The current touch can extend the directional extreme, but the stop remains based on the prior anchor. This needs no future-confirmed pivot. Orders are active only on the next3 observed bars, then expire; they are not repriced. One pending order or position per symbol. At each next open, cancel a pending order if opening price is on/beyond its SL; otherwise a gap beyond the entry stop fills at open, or intrabar trigger fills at entry stop. Risk is actual entry to fixed SL; target is actual entry plus/minus exactly that risk. No partial/trailing/breakeven/time stop. Episode reset can cancel still-pending orders only after that bar's execution, because the reset uses the bar close. Signal while occupied is consumed but ignored. No new order on an exit bar. The strategy decision stream is independent of occupancy.

For an existing position, adverse stop gaps fill at open; favorable target gaps fill at the target without favorable improvement. Within OHLC, SL precedes TP if both hit. On the entry bar, if its full range touches both entry and SL, conservatively charge SL regardless of unknown intrabar ordering. No sub-bar accuracy claim. Unclosed positions at phase boundary are valued and liquidated at last observed close; this research-only exception is flagged separately and excluded sensitivity is reported. Stop orders cannot execute on their own signal bar. Entry timestamp is bucket first timestamp for opening fills, bucket end for intrabar accounting (actual instant unknown); signal availability is bucket end, never last observed minute for a partial bucket. Holding cost conservatively uses ceil((exit exposure end-entry bucket first)/86400).

## Costs, evaluation and reproducibility

All11 symbols share rules and equal initial R. Before strategy results, the data audit quarantined CHFJPY/EURJPY/GBPJPY/USDCHF due to Bars/tick sample mismatches (GBPJPY also has a later scale anomaly); see data_inventory.md. The common indicator input begins2009-10-01. Training/validation now require8 profitable symbols and final6; all other thresholds and 4-candidate budget remain fixed. This is a normalized research score, not an account return or conversion to JPY/USD balance. Two primary roundtrip costs: fixed1.8pips and metadata spread+0.4pips. Metadata commission must be zero. Fixed1.8 represents assumed1.4spread+0.4slippage; no observed slippage claim. Subtract cost once from gross P/L in R. Add adverse holding charge0.2pip/calendar day; report0 and1pip/day sensitivities. Real swap is unverified and Bid evidence is based on sparse source samples only. A 1-pip/day failure prohibits live-ready claims.

Periods, train selection, thresholds and comparison improvement are in protocol.json. Train only selects one candidate; validation only sees that candidate and its matching baseline; final only sees that same pair after validation passes. Failure ends this experiment, no fallback. Later data have been used in prior project research, hence cannot be described as untouched OOS. All thresholds are screening choices: modest positive margin with frequency, currency breadth and DD restrictions, not evidence of guaranteed profitability. Require strict first-only PF and mean-R improvement in both costs in training and validation to justify the extra first-only state. The final confirmation additionally applies the standalone frozen gates.

Reproduce: `python -m pytest tests/test_holy_grail_first_pullback.py -q`; `python -m scripts.research_holy_grail_first_pullback --phase training`. After independent selection review only, validator invokes `--phase validation` and then `--phase final_confirmation` using the same output path. Default output is `agent_runs/holy_grail_first_pullback_20260913/completed`; raw inputs are read-only. Code/spec/dependency hashes and phase-bounded source hashes are saved.
