# Confirmed setup, favorable pullback limit: finite new experiment

Prior experiment `bb3_context_timing_20260909` rejected every market-entry
timing alternative. Training-only diagnostics found baseline mean gross
+0.0584 pips versus modeled 1.4-pip round-trip cost, and delayed confirmations
reduced gross expectancy further. A favorable pullback limit may preserve
reversion distance without chasing the reversal. It can fail because only
adverse continuations fill while successful immediate reversals leave unfilled
orders. This adverse selection is the main falsifiable risk, not a promise.

Keep original Pine baseline signals, dynamic SMA20 full exit, no stop, costs,
and all 9 symbols unchanged. Change ONLY post-signal entry order timing/price.
Exactly six limit variants plus one original market baseline. No result-driven
parameter expansion. Frozen training selection then baseline/selected only
validation and previously viewed final year; no true unseen data claim.

Conservative OHLC assumption: a limit filled intrabar and a central target also
touched in that bar does not prove their order. Hold that trade through this
bar and update target at close. If entry occurs at the opening gap, target
execution later in that bar is allowed. Count ambiguous bars. This is a
specified deterministic convention, not a tick-level worst-case proof.

Pips across quote currencies are an equal-pip research score, not money.
No variable spread, swaps, queue priority, liquidity, partial fills or broker
constraints are modeled. Limit touch is an ideal fill assumption. Any promising
result still needs independent checking and live TradingView verification.
