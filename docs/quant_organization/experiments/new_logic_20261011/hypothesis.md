## Common causal measurements

At signal index i require complete bars i-25 through i and timestamps exactly 900 seconds apart. For preceding 24 bars i-24..i-1 freeze U=max(high), L=min(low), W=U-L, M=(U+L)/2. ATR is simple mean of 20 true ranges i-20..i-1, with prior close available; true range=max(high-low, abs(high-previous close), abs(low-previous close)). Efficiency=abs(close[i-1]-close[i-25])/sum(abs(diff(close[i-25..i-1]))), zero movement means efficiency zero. W and ATR must be positive. This is a rolling box, never retroactively repositioned. All episode state resets at a gap, incomplete bar, or phase boundary. Indicators may warm up on prior data; orders and positions may not.

## A: compression breakout, first retest held

Hypothesis: contraction followed by a directional displacement can reveal a new short-term price equilibrium; its first successful boundary retest may provide a nearby structural invalidation while leaving continuation room. This can fail through false breaks, an extended impulse before the retest, no continuation, or transaction costs exceeding the small stop.

Arm long when W<=3*ATR, close[i]>U, close[i]>open[i], and close[i]-open[i]>=0.5*ATR; short mirrors below L with negative body. Freeze box and ATR. The retest signal risk ceiling uses this breakout-time frozen ATR, not a later recalculation. Pending bars also require the same 26-bar complete contiguous window. Boundary close equality invalidates the episode. No replacement arm while pending. The breakout bar cannot be the retest. Examine the next six complete contiguous bars. The first touch of U from above (low<=U; short high>=L) consumes the episode, even if unsuccessful. Valid long retest requires close>U and close>open; short close<L and close<open. Stop is retest low minus one metadata tick (short high plus tick). If close moves back inside the box before/at touch without valid confirmation, cancel; if no touch by bar six, cancel. No later retry for that arm. Pending state is independent of trade occupancy; signals during a position are consumed and skipped.

Control A0 uses the identical compression breakout bar directly as the signal, with that bar's opposite extreme plus one tick as SL, without a retest. A0 uses exactly the same arm opportunities as A: no new A0 signal while the shared A episode is pending, and no new arm on its consuming/expiring bar. It measures the value of the whole retest entry policy, not a pure causal treatment effect because SL and entry time change.

## B: failed range excursion

Hypothesis: a bounded, directionally inefficient price sequence followed by a failed outside excursion can reveal rejected liquidity-taking; return toward the old interior may be more likely than extension. This is a price-box hypothesis, not evidence that stops/liquidity were actually observed. It fails during new directional information, repeated trends masquerading as ranges, or an excursion too wide for the remaining interior distance.

Require 3*ATR<=W<=8*ATR and efficiency<=0.25. Long signal: low[i]<L, L<close[i]<M, close[i]>open[i], and high[i]<U. Short: high[i]>U, M<close[i]<U, close[i]<open[i], low[i]>L. A bar sweeping both edges is rejected. SL=signal low-tick for long/high+tick for short. Freeze M as the available recovery boundary. At entry the the 2R target must lie at or before M for every RR configuration; otherwise skip. Each signal must be separated from the previous signal of the same B arm by at least 24 M15 bars, including skipped/occupied signals, to avoid repeatedly counting one range excursion.

Control B0 keeps the range, candle, room, cooldown and SL rules but substitutes an inside boundary touch: long L<=low<=L+0.1*W; short U-0.1*W<=high<=U, with strict opposite edge not touched. It measures whether outside rejection adds value relative to an ordinary inside edge bounce, not whether all mean reversion works.

## Entries, exits and costs

Signal is available at its confirmed close; enter only the next exact contiguous M15 open backed by an actual source M1 at that open. Only at that open check risk=side*(entry-SL)>0, risk>=10*normal round-trip cost, and risk<=2*signal ATR. B also checks side*(M-entry)>=2*risk. These checks use opening price only, not the entry candle's later OHLC. Fixed TP=entry+side*RR*risk for RR in [1,1.5,2], full exit, no partial/breakeven/trailing exit. Normal/stress use identical entry eligibility (normal-cost floor); stress doubles actual charged cost only. Signals, initial entry eligibility, and structural SL are common across RR levels; occupancy can change realized trade sets because longer targets hold positions longer.

At most one position per symbol/arm/cost. At entry and each subsequent candle, adverse opening gaps fill at open; favorable TP gaps fill at target, SL first when both touched. An existing position encountering a missing interval closes at the next observed open (favorable price capped at TP); incomplete execution bars check protective exits then close at observed close if still open. Other full exits: 16th held M15 close, source-day final 23:45 close, and phase-final bar close. No entry on an already occupied bar, including its exit bar. A weekend gap may exceed nominal risk; no fixed maximum loss claim.

Costs, per round trip: USDJPY 1.6 pip, EURUSD 1.5 pip, GBPUSD 1.8 pip (metadata spread plus 0.2 pip slippage each side), stress twice these. Commission zero assumption. Swap not included: source-day clearance and 4h maximum limit overnight exposure but actual broker rollover clock is unknown. Source time/side unverified, OHLC not bid/ask/tick execution. No precision claim beyond conservative bars.
